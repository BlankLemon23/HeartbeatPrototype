gameprototype
Windows play guide

Extract the complete ZIP to a folder and open gameprototype.exe.
Keep level.txt in the same folder as the executable.

GOAL
Survive and defeat all enemies. The pistol can handle an isolated patrol.
Save ammo for stragglers or a distraction when you face a group.

CONTROLS
W / A / S / D   Move
Left Shift      Toggle stealth
Space           Scan while exploring
Left mouse      Fire the pistol, or throw the selected item
R               Reload
V / B           Select or cancel Molotov / decoy mine
1 / 2           Craft a Molotov / decoy mine
Esc             Exit
The result screen offers Restart and Quit.

READ THE MAP
- Scan to reveal terrain and supplies within line of sight. Revealed
  terrain stays on the map. The scan reaches up to 11 cells. Yellow
  squares show where enemies were at scan time. They disappear after
  about one second.
- The player square is blue in normal movement and purple in stealth.
  Stealth slows movement and reduces enemy sight range from six cells
  to three. The blue outline is a distance guide. Walls block sight.
- Scans happen automatically in combat and become more frequent as
  health falls. Break away until the HUD returns to EXPLORING before
  trying to craft or scan manually.

DRAW ENEMIES AWAY
- This prototype is silent. An actual pistol shot still creates in-game
  noise at your position, even when it misses or hits a wall. You can
  spend a round to draw enemies within nine cells toward your old
  position, then move. The red aim line shows maximum range. Walls
  can stop the shot early.
- A decoy mine creates one noise event as soon as it lands. Throw it
  away from your route to pull patrols within ten cells toward it.
  Noise can carry through walls. Enemies take walkable paths to it.
  Enemies already chasing you ignore the distraction.
- Scan from cover and give enemies time to converge. While they are
  investigating, you can circle behind them to reach guarded supplies.

BURN AND RESUPPLY
- Throw a Molotov into the gathered group from a safe distance. Orange
  cells show the fire area. Fire reaches up to four cells and damages
  enemies and the player over time. Walls block the spread of fire.
  After a successful throw, the next click fires the pistol again.
- The throw outline uses terrain you have already scanned. If an
  unscanned wall blocks the throw, the item lands before the wall.
  Scanning the intended route first makes throws more predictable.
- Walk over green M markers for materials and blue A markers for ammo.
  Craft outside combat. A Molotov costs 3 materials and a decoy costs 2.
  Save materials for the next group and reload in cover.
